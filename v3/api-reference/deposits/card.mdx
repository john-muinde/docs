---
title: "Card Deposit"
openapi: "POST /api/v3/deposit/card"
---

<Note>
Since 18 November 2026 card details are no longer accepted. Do not send `card`: a request that includes card details is refused with a 400. The response's `redirectUrl` is a hosted checkout page; send the customer there to enter their card.
</Note>

Starts a card payment. The customer enters their card on a secure hosted checkout page, where 3-D Secure also runs, so card numbers never pass through your systems.

1. Call this endpoint with the amount, currency, customer name and phone, and your `referenceId`.
2. Send the customer to the `redirectUrl` in the response.
3. When they finish, they return to your `customerRedirectUrl` if you set one.
4. Track the outcome with the [card deposit status](/v3/api-reference/deposits/card-status) endpoint or your callback URL.
