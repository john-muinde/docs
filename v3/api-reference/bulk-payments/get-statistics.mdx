---
title: "Get Bulk Payment Statistics"
openapi: "GET /api/v3/dashboard/bulk-payments/api/stats"
---

Get statistics and analytics for bulk payments.

Payments are counted as successful (`totalSuccessfulPayments`), failed (`totalFailedPayments`) or still pending (`totalPendingPayments`). `successRate` is the share of successful payments among the resolved ones, so payments still in progress do not lower it.
