---
title: "Get Withdraw Service Configuration"
openapi: "GET /api/v3/customer/support/services/withdraw"
---

Get the countries where withdrawals are available: for each, the currency, whether the service is active, whether it is enabled for your account (`isEnabled`), and whether a network or account name must be sent with a payout there. For the cost of a payout, see [Rates](/v3/api-reference/rates/offramp-rate) and [Fees & Billing](/v3/essentials/billing-types).
