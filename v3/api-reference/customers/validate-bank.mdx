---
title: "Validate Bank Account"
openapi: "POST /api/v3/customer/validate/bank"
---

Checks a bank account before you pay it: the account number format, the bank
code, and whether the country is supported.

If your account is enabled for **name lookup**, the response also carries the
name actually registered on the account (`accountName`), its status
(`accountStatus` — `ACTIVE`, `FROZEN` or `RESTRICTED`), and `nameLookupStatus`.
Without that entitlement you still get the format and corridor checks, and
`nameLookupStatus` comes back as `NOT_ENABLED` — the request is identical either
way.

Whenever a lookup does not return a name, `nameLookupReason` says why, so you can
tell an account that does not exist (`NOT_FOUND`) from a corridor that cannot
resolve names at all (`UNSUPPORTED`) from something worth retrying (`ERROR`,
`TIMEOUT`).

`isValid` reports the account number format, except on `NOT_FOUND` — once the
bank tells us the account does not exist, `isValid` comes back `false` too. A
name *mismatch* is different: the account is real, so `isValid` stays `true` and
`nameMatch.matched` is what tells you the holder is not who you expected. See
[which field answers which question](/v3/essentials/account-validation#which-field-answers-which-question).

Send `accountName` with the name you expect on the account and the response adds
a `nameMatch` block scoring it against the real one, so you can catch a wrong
account number before the money moves.

<Tip>
Use the [validation test values](/v3/testing/account-validation)
to reproduce frozen, unresolvable and mismatched-name accounts on demand.
</Tip>
