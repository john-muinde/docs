---
title: "Onramp: Buy Crypto"
description: "Take local currency and deliver crypto to a wallet."
---

An onramp converts local fiat currency into cryptocurrency. The customer pays via mobile money or bank checkout; once payment confirms, Kotani Pay sends the crypto to the wallet address you provided.

<Tip>
Don't want to prefund? With [hosted checkout](/v3/flows/hosted-checkout-onramp) the customer pays on a hosted page and we deliver the crypto, with every fee shown in the quote before they pay.
</Tip>

---

<Note>
You need [an API key](/v3/getting-started/api-keys) and a reachable `callbackUrl` — see the [Quickstart](/v3/quickstart) if you haven't set those up. For this flow you also need a [customer record](/v3/api-reference/customers/mobile-money/create) for the person paying, and the crypto wallet address to deliver to.
</Note>

## 1. Get a Rate Quote

Fetch the current exchange rate before initiating so you can show the customer what crypto amount they'll receive. Call [Get Onramp Exchange Rate](/v3/api-reference/rates/onramp-rate) with the fiat currency and crypto token.

---

## 2. Initiate the Onramp

Call [Create Onramp](/v3/api-reference/onramp/create) with your `callbackUrl` and the destination crypto wallet address. The response returns `id`, `referenceId`, `referenceNumber`, `message`, `customerKey`, `redirectUrl` and `hosted`. The rate is locked only when you pass a `rateId` from the rate quote in step 1, and a `rateId` is valid for 60 seconds.

<Note>
For currencies shared by several countries (XOF, XAF), the payment is collected in the phone number's country. You can also send `country` to state it. See [Which country the payment is collected in](/v3/api-reference/onramp/create#which-country-the-payment-is-collected-in).
</Note>

---

## 3. Customer Pays

- **Mobile money** — the customer receives an STK push (M-PESA, Airtel) or USSD prompt (MTN, others) and confirms by entering their PIN
- **Bank checkout**: the response includes a `redirectUrl`; redirect the customer there to complete payment on the bank's page

The customer has not paid yet when you receive the initial response — they must complete their confirmation step.

---

## 4. Crypto is Delivered

Onramp has two independent status fields, `depositStatus` (the fiat payment) and `onchainStatus` (the crypto delivery). Once fiat payment confirms, Kotani Pay sends the crypto to the wallet:

| `depositStatus` | `onchainStatus` | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `PENDING` or `INITIATED` | `PENDING` | Waiting for the customer to pay |
| `SUCCESSFUL` | `PENDING` or `IN_PROGRESS` | Fiat confirmed, crypto transfer in progress |
| `SUCCESSFUL` | `SUCCESSFUL` | Crypto delivered (`transactionHash` is set) |
| `SUCCESSFUL` | `FAILED` | Fiat collected but the crypto transfer failed |
| `FAILED` or `CANCELLED` | `FAILED` | The customer did not pay |

See [Transaction Statuses](/v3/essentials/transaction-statuses) for every value.

<Warning>
If the crypto transfer fails after fiat is collected, it is retried once automatically. If it fails again, it needs support. The fiat stays credited to your deposit balance; it is not refunded automatically.
</Warning>

---

## 5. Kotani Pay Notifies You

Kotani Pay POSTs to your `callbackUrl` once the deposit is `SUCCESSFUL`, `FAILED` or `CANCELLED` and the on-chain leg is `SUCCESSFUL` or `FAILED`:

- `depositStatus` `SUCCESSFUL` and `onchainStatus` `SUCCESSFUL`: crypto delivered to the wallet
- `onchainStatus` `FAILED`: the crypto was not delivered (either the customer did not pay, or the transfer failed after fiat was collected)

<Note>
If you have a webhook secret configured, the callback arrives in a signed envelope with `X-Kotani-Signature`, `X-Kotani-Event`, and `X-Kotani-Integrator` headers. Without a secret, the transaction fields are posted directly to your `callbackUrl`. See [Webhooks](/v3/essentials/webhooks) for both modes, and the [onramp payload](/v3/essentials/webhooks#transactiononrampstatusupdated) for every field you receive with a worked example.
</Note>
